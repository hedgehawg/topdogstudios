The firm

Prediction markets are becoming real financial infrastructure: regulated exchanges where the underlying is a probability. Top Dog Studios provides the service every young market needs most. Continuous, two-sided, honestly priced liquidity.

We trade solely the firm's own capital, using automated quantitative strategies on regulated event-contract exchanges. Two-sided market making and cross-venue arbitrage. No customers, no outside investors, no third-party funds.

The craft is as old as markets. The venue is new. That combination is the firm.

04

The market

Every new market in history has needed professional liquidity before it could grow up. That is the seat we are taking.

The 2024 U.S. election proved demand at scale. Event contracts went from curiosity to front-page price discovery in a single cycle. Courts affirmed the CFTC-supervised exchanges' right to list them. Retail brokerages now distribute them beside stocks and options. What the asset class still lacks is depth: tight, reliable markets across thousands of contracts, most of which trade thinly or not at all.

That gap is the business. Not a commentary shop. A market-maker already in place when event risk becomes a standard institutional hedge.

Research

Two public studies sit behind the work. The math is the same instinct as the desk: look at the shape of a system, not the story people tell about it.

Topology · Language

Topology of Societal Decoherence

As social feeds pull left and right toward different words, the two sides slowly stop sharing a language. This is a visualization. Our linguistic feasible space, our respective linguistic universes, are represented as topological manifolds (tori) modeled on findings from a consistent platform: the floor of Congress (1994 through 2025). The right and left have separated like a failed emulsion. The mathematical description is a fiber bundle on a torus, decohering.

Markets · Topological Data Analysis

Gold's Rally: A Topological Contextualization

Is gold's price spike a bubble or the real thing? A math tool that measures the shape of how markets move together. The shape looks completely normal, even though the price move was historic. Translation: a genuine, lasting repricing of gold against paper money, not a panic. For the technical reader: persistent homology of cross-asset correlations, 43rd-percentile normal.
